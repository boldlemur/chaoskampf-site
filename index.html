About

Order from chaos.

Chaoskampf is an independent development studio based in the United Kingdom. We build iOS applications.

Chaoskampf is a German word for the struggle against chaos — a recurring motif in ancient mythology. It seemed like a reasonable name for a software company.

We're a small team, working on a small number of things at once.
