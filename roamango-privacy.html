Privacy Policy

Roamango

Last updated: 11 July 2026

Roamango is an iOS app by Chaoskampf that turns your photo library into animated travel reels. It is built to be private by design: there are no accounts, no ads, and no Chaoskampf servers. Your photos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to us — we could not see them even if we wanted to.

Your photos

Roamango requests read-only access to your photo library through Apple's PhotoKit framework. Scanning, trip detection, photo scoring, and reel rendering all happen on your device. The app never modifies your library, and neither your photos nor their metadata are transmitted to Chaoskampf. Finished reels are saved on your device and leave it only when you choose to share them.

Location data in your photos

The app reads the GPS metadata embedded in your photos to detect where you have travelled. This processing happens entirely on your device. The places you have visited are not sent to Chaoskampf. The two narrow exceptions — map imagery and place-name lookups — are described below.

Maps

Map imagery is provided by Mapbox. When you view a map or create a reel, the app requests map tiles from Mapbox's servers. Like any web request, these include your IP address, and the map areas requested correspond to the regions shown on screen. The Mapbox SDK may also collect anonymised usage telemetry to improve its maps; you can opt out via the telemetry setting in the map's attribution (ⓘ) menu.

Place names

To name the places in your reels, Roamango first uses an offline place database bundled with the app. If no match is found there, the app may ask Apple's geocoding service for a name, which involves sending approximate coordinates to Apple. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles such requests.

Analytics

We use TelemetryDeck to collect anonymous usage signals — for example, that a reel was created or a setting was changed. These signals contain no personal identifiers and never include your photos, their metadata, your location, or the places you have travelled. TelemetryDeck cannot track you across apps.

Device location

Roamango can optionally use your device's location to show where you are on the map. This requires your explicit permission, is used only to display your position, and is never stored or transmitted.

Data storage and deletion

Everything Roamango knows — detected trips, settings, caches — is stored on your device. There is nothing to delete on any server: removing the app removes all of its data.

Beta versions

If you use a beta version of Roamango through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares with us any feedback you choose to send, along with crash logs and basic device information, as described in the TestFlight terms presented when you join.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated revision date.
